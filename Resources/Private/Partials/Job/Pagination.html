The navigation between the pages of a paginated job list. Every link carries the page it leads to, and nothing else: the job type and the other settings of the list come from its content element. The section "PageLink" renders one. Its text is the page number, or the label "labelKey" names for first, previous, next and last. "hasLessPages" and "hasMorePages" exist on the numbered pagination only: the core pagination links every page and renders no ellipsis. Arguments: - paginator: the paginator, for the current page - pagination: the pagination, for the pages to link
